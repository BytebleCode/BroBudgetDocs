BroBudget

A private budget that lives on your iPhone.

  • Transactions come straight from your bank through a read-only connection you control (SimpleFIN or your own Plaid keys), or from files you import.
  • Spending is categorized on your iPhone by rules and a model that learns from your corrections.
  • Budgets, forecasts and spending alerts, all calculated on the device.
  • Encrypted with a key sealed by the Secure Enclave. No accounts, servers, ads or tracking.
