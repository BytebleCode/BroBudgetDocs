Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Short version: BroBudget doesn't collect any data. There are no BroBudget accounts, servers, analytics, ads or trackers. Your financial data stays on your iPhone, encrypted.

What BroBudget stores, and where

Your accounts, balances, transactions, categories, budgets, rules and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are encrypted with AES-256, using a key sealed by your iPhone's Secure Enclave, and are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. The developer of BroBudget never receives, sees or stores any of it.

Deleting the app, or choosing Settings → Delete all data, permanently erases this data and the keys that protect it.

Bank connections you choose to set up

BroBudget can download read-only bank data through a provider you sign up for yourself. Your iPhone talks to that provider directly; nothing passes through any server run by BroBudget's developer.

  • SimpleFIN Bridge. You create a SimpleFIN account and connect your banks on their website. BroBudget stores the access key SimpleFIN issues in your iPhone's Keychain. See SimpleFIN's privacy policy.
  • Plaid, with your own keys. You create your own Plaid developer account and enter its keys in the app. You sign in to your bank on Plaid's own web page; BroBudget never sees your bank password. Your Plaid keys and bank access tokens are stored in your iPhone's Keychain, sealed by the Secure Enclave. See Plaid's end user privacy policy.

Those providers handle your data under their own privacy policies. Removing a connection in BroBudget deletes its credentials from your iPhone and, for Plaid, asks Plaid to revoke access.

Files you import

CSV, OFX, QFX and QBO files you import are read on your iPhone and are never uploaded.

Categorization and AI

Transactions are categorized on your iPhone by built-in rules and a model that learns from your corrections. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, BroBudget can also ask Apple's on-device language model for help with uncertain transactions. That processing happens on your iPhone; BroBudget sends nothing to any AI service.

Notifications

Spending alerts are created and scheduled on your iPhone. BroBudget doesn't use push notification servers.

Exports

If you export your transactions to a CSV file, you choose where that file goes. BroBudget doesn't send it anywhere on its own.

Children

BroBudget isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
