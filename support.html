How do I get my transactions in?

Go to Settings → Add a bank or file and pick one:

  • SimpleFIN Bridge (easiest). Sign up at SimpleFIN, connect your banks there, then paste the one-time setup token into BroBudget. SimpleFIN charges its own small subscription.
  • Plaid with your own keys (free). Create a free Plaid developer account. BroBudget links you to each page you need and shows exactly which two keys to copy. Plaid's free Trial plan covers up to 10 bank logins.
  • Import a file (offline). Download a CSV, OFX, QFX or QBO file from your bank's website and import it. Importing an overlapping file later won't create duplicates.

You can mix these. Just don't connect the same bank twice, or its transactions will appear twice.

A transaction has the wrong category

Tap it and choose the right category. BroBudget learns from the change and can apply it to similar purchases from the same merchant.

My bank says it needs me to sign in again

Banks sometimes expire connections. For Plaid, tap Reconnect under the bank in Settings; this doesn't use up another Trial bank login. For SimpleFIN, sign in again on the SimpleFIN Bridge website.

How far back does history go?

Up to two years for newly connected banks, depending on what your bank provides. You can change this under Settings → History for new banks before connecting.

Can I keep an account out of my totals?

Yes. Tap the accounts button at the top of the dashboard and switch off any account, such as a business card. You can rename accounts there too.

Is my data backed up?

No. For privacy, BroBudget's data is excluded from iCloud and computer backups and lives only on this iPhone. Use Settings → Export transactions (CSV) to keep your own copy.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all data erases every transaction, budget, rule and stored key. Deleting the app does the same.
